🛠️ Beyond Box Admin Panel

Complete Operational Manual & Feature Architecture of all 22 Platform Modules

🌐 Live Admin: /admin ⚡ Sub-second Speed: 0.16s - 0.30s 🏬 Multi-Vendor Ready 🛡️ Role-Based Access (RBAC)

🧭 Executive Overview

The Beyond Box Admin Panel gives non-technical store managers, operations executives, and corporate admins complete 360° control over inventory, orders, marketing campaigns, vendor payouts, and checkout rules with zero code dependencies.

  • Unified Global Search
  • Instant PDF Tax Invoicing
  • Automatic WebP Media Optimization
  • Real-Time Courier Tracking (AWB)
  • Granular Staff Permissions (RBAC)
  • Multi-Gateway Online & COD Payments

📋 Complete 22 Admin Panel Modules Breakdown

1. 📊 Dashboard

/admin
Central executive command center and real-time business health monitoring station.
  • Live KPI Metric Cards: Real-time counters for Total Orders, Active Catalog Products (30 verified items), Registered Buyers, and Reviews.
  • Sales Analytics Charts: Interactive graphical visualization of monthly revenue breakdowns and daily order velocity.
  • Recent Orders Quick Actions: Review incoming orders with instant shortcuts to view details, verify payments, or dispatch.
  • System Audit Log (/admin/audit-logs): Comprehensive compliance audit recording who edited prices, created products, or changed settings with timestamps and IP addresses.

2. 🛍️ Ecommerce

#cms-plugins-ecommerce
The core commercial engine powering daily catalog management, orders, discounts, and customer checkout.
  • Products Management: Create Simple Products (single SKU) and Variable Products (with Size, Color, Storage attributes). Schedule promotional sales with automated calendar timers.
  • Inventory & Stock Control: Automatic deduction on order placement, low-stock warnings, and backorder handling.
  • Categories Tree: Unlimited hierarchical parent-child categories with custom category banners that trigger the Beyond Box Logo Blue theme (#012d84).
  • Brands Directory: Dedicated brand showcase pages (e.g. /brands) for Apple, Sony, Samsung, Bose.
  • Order Fulfillment Pipeline: 5-stage lifecycle (Pending ➔ Processing ➔ Shipped ➔ Completed) with courier assignment and live tracking AWB links.
  • Automated PDF Invoices: Generate professional GST/Tax invoices and printable packing slips with one click.
  • Marketing Discounts & Coupons: Create percentage off (SAVE20), flat off (FLAT500), or free shipping coupons with minimum order values and usage limits.
  • Flash Sales: Run time-sensitive sales with real-time countdown clocks on storefront cards.
  • Reviews Moderation: Approve customer ratings with "Verified Purchase" badges.

3. 📑 Product Specification

#cms-plugins-product-specification
Technical data sheets and attributes manager for electronics, gadgets, and technical products.
  • Create grouped technical tables (e.g. Processor, Display, Battery, Connectivity, Dimensions, Warranty).
  • Neatly presented in a dedicated "Specifications" tab on the product detail page for customer clarity.

4. 🏬 Marketplace

#cms-plugins-marketplace
Transforms the platform into a multi-seller ecosystem (Amazon / Flipkart model).
  • Vendor Onboarding & KYC: External sellers register online; admin verifies business documents and approves/rejects vendor stores.
  • Automated Commission Engine: Set global platform commission percentages or vendor-specific rates on every completed sale.
  • Vendor Wallets & Payouts: Automated net earnings calculation; sellers submit payout requests for admin bank/PayPal transfer.
  • Individual Store Pages: Dedicated vendor storefront profile (/stores/vendor-name) showcasing the vendor's catalog.

5. 📄 Pages

/admin/pages
Content Management System (CMS) for informational and corporate landing pages.
  • Manage Homepage, About Us, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Return & Refund Policy, Shipping Information.
  • Full WYSIWYG editor with visual formatting and custom SEO Meta Titles/Descriptions for top Google search indexing.

6. ✍️ Blog

#cms-plugins-blog
Content marketing and organic Google SEO traffic generation engine.
  • Publish buyer guides, tech news, unboxing reviews, and promotional articles.
  • Organize posts into categories and tags to attract high-intent organic shoppers directly from search engines.

7. 💳 Payments

#cms-plugins-payments
Comprehensive financial audit trail and transaction log.
  • Tracks every online and offline payment attempt (Success, Pending, Failed, Refunded).
  • Captures payment gateway reference IDs (Stripe Charge ID, PayPal Order ID, Razorpay Payment ID) for reconciliation.

8. 🖼️ Galleries

/admin/galleries
Interactive visual media galleries and lookbook albums.
  • Create photo albums for corporate lookbooks, warehouse operations, product launches, or customer events.
  • Easily embed albums into any page with clean shortcodes.

9. ⭐ Testimonials

/admin/testimonials
Social proof and client feedback showcase.
  • Manage customer name, avatar photo, company/designation, star rating, and review quote.
  • Automatically feeds the "What Our Clients Say" carousel slider on the homepage.

10. 📢 Ads

#cms-plugins-ads
On-site banner advertising and promotional placement management.
  • Upload promotional banners displayed in headers, category headers, sidebars, and homepage slots.
  • Set destination URLs, target window settings, and monitor impressions and click-through rates.

11. 🔔 Announcements

/admin/announcements
Topbar notification ribbon at the very head of the website.
  • Display urgent notices (e.g. "Free Shipping on orders above $99" or "Flash Sale Live — Code: BEYOND20").
  • Supports live countdown timers, CTA buttons, and dismissible close actions.

12. 📬 Contact

#cms-plugins-contact
Inquiry inbox capturing messages submitted through the /contact page.
  • Logs sender name, email, phone number, subject, and message body.
  • Maintains status tracking (Unread, Read, Replied) to ensure prompt customer support.

13. 🎞️ Simple Sliders

/admin/simple-sliders
Homepage Hero Carousel banner slider management.
  • Control the first high-impact visual banners visitors see on the homepage.
  • Update background graphics, headlines, promotional subtitles, and call-to-action buttons (e.g. "Shop Now").

14. ❓ FAQs

#cms-plugins-faq
Frequently Asked Questions knowledge base to streamline customer support.
  • Group questions into categories (Shipping, Returns, Payments, Account).
  • Displays in an interactive accordion (+/- expand) on the /faq page and footer.

15. 📧 Newsletters

/admin/newsletters
Email marketing subscriber database.
  • Stores subscriber email addresses captured from the footer signup form.
  • One-click export to CSV/Excel for campaigns via Mailchimp, SendGrid, or Brevo.

16. 🌍 Locations

#cms-plugins-location
Global shipping geographical database (Countries, States, Cities).
  • Define supported countries and regions where shipping is available.
  • Powers dynamic checkout address dropdowns and location-based shipping fee calculations.

17. 📁 Media

/admin/media
Built-in Cloud Storage Drive (in-app Google Drive / Dropbox).
  • Drag-and-drop bulk file upload with folder organization (main/products, main/product-categories).
  • Automated WebP conversion and responsive thumbnail generation to keep site speed lightning fast.

18. 🎨 Appearance

#cms-core-appearance
Visual branding, theme options, and navigation hierarchy.
  • Theme Options (/admin/theme/options): Change Header Logo, Mobile Logo, Admin Logo, Favicon, Topbar notices, Footer text, and Brand color palette.
  • Menus (/admin/menus): Visual drag-and-drop builder for the Main Navbar, Category Dropdown, and Footer links.

19. 🧩 Plugins

#cms-core-plugins
Modular platform feature toggle manager.
  • Enable or disable platform modules (e.g. Marketplace, Blog, FAQ, Newsletter) with a single toggle switch without touching code.

20. 🔧 Tools

#cms-core-tools
Data Synchronize and bulk import/export center.
  • Bulk import thousands of products, inventory stocks, prices, and categories via Excel/CSV.
  • Export data for accounting, warehouse management, or ERP integration.

21. ⚙️ Settings

/admin/settings
Global system configuration engine.
  • Payment Gateways: Configure Cash on Delivery (COD), Direct Bank Transfer, Stripe, PayPal, and Razorpay API credentials.
  • Shipping Methods: Set free shipping cart thresholds, flat rates, and regional delivery charges.
  • Email & SMTP: Automated email notifications for order placements, invoices, and shipment tracking via Gmail/SendGrid/Amazon SES.
  • Currencies: Multi-currency support (USD, INR, AED, EUR) with exchange rates and formatting.

22. 🛡️ Platform Administration

/admin/system
System health, server maintenance, and role-based staff security (RBAC).
  • Users & Roles: Create staff accounts (e.g. Order Manager, Inventory Clerk, Support Executive) with restricted permission access.
  • 1-Click System Backups: Download complete database .sql files and uploaded media .zip archives directly to your computer.
  • Cache Clearing: Clear route, config, and view caches with one click.
